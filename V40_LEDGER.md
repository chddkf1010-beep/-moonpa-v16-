# v40 phase 3 — persistent player cash journal

Retains the existing monthly report and temporary engine capture. The same actual Ledger._record transaction writes a separate player-only journal, including outside-month FA, transfers and facility transactions. Opening at founding or old-save import (no fictional earlier history). Full income/expense totals and category totals by season; latest 120 detailed entries, numbered and with post-transaction balance. The cash equation is checked on save; NPC money is not included, debt is separate, and successor capital after dissolution is excluded. UI under Records > Cumulative ledger, showing seasonal cash flows, categories and recent filters; fixed-cost ratio is forecast, not realized return. No new spending or simulation rules. Old web snapshots initialize the journal at their existing balance; local v39 save migration remains separate. Main is unchanged.
