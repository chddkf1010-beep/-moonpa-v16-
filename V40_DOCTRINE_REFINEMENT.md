Candidate selection and evaluation use the same three symmetric roster presets, not an independent holdout: 540 battles per ruleset (six terrains, fifteen doctrine pairs, both side assignments and three rosters). Not a guarantee of campaign-wide balance. Old-rule compatibility and new replay settlement checked. Main not deployed.
