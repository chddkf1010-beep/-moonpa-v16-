# v40 phase 6b — season cup

49/50/51 weeks: quarterfinals/semifinals/finals in each of four divisions, 28 matches total. Seed based on existing season ranking at week 49. Healthy top eight default, real existing group engine (NPC auto-resolver; non-browser fallback power comparison). If no healthy fighters, forfeit. Player can watch, manually adjust lineup via existing arena bench or skip. Real combat XP and injuries, participating members excluded from training that week. During cup weeks regular attacks are suspended for all guilds, economic settlement continues. One win = 100 points, maximum 300. After week 52 normal score is normalized relative to the highest regular score in each division (maximum 700); combined score determines existing annual ranking, awards and playoff pairs. Original tie-breaks maintained. Persist latest cup state as optional world field. Old saves loaded after week 49 with no cup continue legacy season, no retroactive matches. No extra prizes, items or new skills. Existing main remains unchanged.
